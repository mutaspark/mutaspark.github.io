Delete your Brainrot Evolution Lab data

Brainrot Evolution Lab, by Mutaspark

The game keeps your data only on your phone

Brainrot Evolution Lab has no accounts and no server: your progress, coins, gems and purchases are stored only on your device. To delete it:

  1. Open the game and tap the gear button (Settings), then tap DELETE MY DATA and confirm, or
  2. uninstall the game.

Rankings (Google Play Games)

If you were signed in to Google Play Games, your scores are part of your Google Play Games profile, which belongs to your Google account. You can hide or delete that profile and its game data in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account & data), or at myaccount.google.com.

Questions

Email mutaspark@gmail.com. We have no copy of your data, so there is nothing we need to delete on our side.
