Privacy Policy

Brainrot Evolution Lab, by Mutaspark · Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information Brainrot Evolution Lab (the "game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made for players of all ages, including children. We designed it to collect as little as possible: we never ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

1. What the game stores

We do not run any server and we do not receive any of your data. Everything the game keeps stays on your device:

InformationWhyWhere
Your game progress (creatures, coins, gems, upgrades, settings)So you can keep playingOn your device, in an encrypted file
Purchase tokens (a scrambled copy)To deliver what you bought exactly onceOn your device

World rankings use Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your score is sent to Google and shown next to your Play Games gamer tag and avatar, which you choose and control in the Play Games app. We only see the public leaderboard, like any other player. We do not receive any payment details: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. With each purchase the game gives Google Play a random number made on your device (not linked to you) so Google can spot fraud.

When the game starts it asks Google's web server for the current time (an empty request, nothing about you is sent), so daily rewards follow the real date. Like any connection, Google sees your IP address.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Because children play the game, every ad request is marked as "child-directed": ads are not personalised, are limited to family-friendly content (rated G), and the game does not collect the Android Advertising ID. Google may still process technical data such as the IP address and device type to deliver ads and prevent fraud; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent message from Google is shown where the law requires it. Ads that give a reward are always optional. You can remove the other ads with the "Remove Ads" purchase.

3. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is no chat and no way to message other players. Rankings show the Google Play Games profile name, which is managed by Google (including Google's settings for children's accounts). In-game purchases use Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases (Google Family Link / purchase authentication).

4. Services we use

  • Google Play Games Services - world rankings (only if you are signed in to Play Games).
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform - ads and consent.
  • Google Play Billing - in-game purchases.

We do not sell or share any information. The game does not send analytics or tracking data to any company.

5. How long we keep it

We keep nothing ourselves. The data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. Scores on Google Play Games are kept by Google under Google's privacy policy.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: in the game open Settings > DELETE MY DATA, or uninstall the game. See how to delete your data.
  • Rankings: you can play without signing in to Google Play Games; then no score leaves your device. Your Play Games profile, its visibility and its data can be changed or deleted in the Google Play Games app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws) you may also have other rights, such as complaining to your data protection authority.

7. Security

Your save file is encrypted and signed so it cannot be changed, and every purchase is checked against Google's signature before anything is given.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
